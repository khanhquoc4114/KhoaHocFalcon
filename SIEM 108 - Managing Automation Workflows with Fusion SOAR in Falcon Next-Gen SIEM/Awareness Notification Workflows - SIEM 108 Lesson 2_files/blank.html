Tips for Building High-Quality Workflows

Remember the following best practices when building high-quality workflows.

  1. 1

    Use precise filters to avoid unnecessary volume

  2. 2

    Include tags like [AWARENESS] or [INFO-ONLY] in messages

  3. 3

    Test in a limited environment before applying to broad host groups

  4. 4

    Document the workflow's purpose and logic for future audits

When well-constructed, awareness notifications increase team confidence and maintain security visibility, without adding noise. This concludes the lesson.
