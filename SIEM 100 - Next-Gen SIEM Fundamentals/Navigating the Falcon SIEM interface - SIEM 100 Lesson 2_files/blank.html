Monitor and investigate

“The speed of today’s cyberattacks requires security teams to rapidly analyze massive amounts of data to detect, investigate and respond to threats faster." - George Kurtz, CEO and co-founder, CrowdStrike 

Under Monitor and investigate, view and manage events from both CrowdStrike and 3rd-party sources to understand and quickly work through detections and incidents. Learn more about the menu items (Detections, Incidents, Rules, and Detection Coverage) below.

Detections

Review each of the following sections to see some of what you can do under Detections.

  • Apply filters to narrow results

    0:09

    Filter by categories like endpoint and network to quickly see all sources in a consolidated view so you can quickly understand the activity happening in your environment. 

  • Use the MITRE toggle to focus in on tactics and techniques

    0:05

    Apply the MITRE attack matrix filters, which show how the detections are laid into the matrix itself.

  • Quickly find potential threat actor activity

    0:07

    In the detections list, find red-tinted rows with threat actor icons to see any associated threat actors.

  • Review the details panel

    0:10

    Click the row to open a details panel that includes the processes that were involved, hashes, files, hosts, and everything you need to find out what went on in this particular detection.

  • View the process table

    0:09

    On the left, view a process table of what actually occurred leading up to the detection. 


    You can select any of the processes to view more details on that process.

Incidents

Review each of the following sections to see some of what you can do under Incidents.

Video: Incidents and the Incident Workbench

Watch the following video to see some of what you can do under Incidents, such as using the Incident Workbench to improve response time.

Video length: 2:18 minutes

Under Incidents, see a list of any Incidents that have been created. These are events that may not be significant on their own but may be noteworthy when combined with other related processes or detections. 


Like Detections, you can use filters to narrow the list of Incidents and click a row for more details.


Investigate, customize, and manage incidents in the incident workbench by clicking See full incident.


The Incident Workbench can serve as the “single source of truth” when multiple people are involved in the analysis and remediation of an incident. This collaborative workbench provides all of the data and tools that a team of analysts might need when triaging, investigating, and responding to a security incident.


Use the Workbench to see a visual representation of the Incident data.


See exactly how and when an Incident started with the inline timeline widget. Zoom in to see what happened during a specific period of time in the Incident chronology.


Use the legend on the left to interact with the data, see what each icon represents, and focus your investigation. 


Add and manage your own custom nodes, and remove items that aren’t relevant to your investigation. Collaborate with multiple contributors in real time or asynchronously.


Anyone that is online and working on the incident will see updates made by others. Contributors can be tagged in notes. Once the note is saved, a notification email is sent to the tagged user. The tagged user can go directly to the note view in the incident by clicking the link in the email.


Select an item to see details, add notes, see notes that others have added, and take actions such as assigning users, changing incident status or applying tags.


All of the objects are interactive. Select an object to open a panel on the right that includes any known details.


In this Workbench you can:

  • Restore version history in case something is deleted by accident

  • Download an image file

  • Download a PDF and select what is in the report to export and share with others

Rules

Review each of the following sections to see some of what you can do under Rules.

Next-Gen SIEM supports the creation of query-based correlation rules that can generate custom detections or custom incidents when triggered. 

You can:

  • Apply CrowdStrike-provided rule templates.

  • Build your own correlation rules from scratch, based on active engagement with real threats.

Detection coverage

Review each of the following sections to see some of what you can do under Detection coverage.

Detection coverage management

This dashboard displays which MITRE ATT&CK techniques you have coverage for across the Falcon platform (rules that address those techniques), including ingested third-party data from Next-Gen SIEM. 

You can:

  • Visualize your detection and MITRE ATT&CK Tactics & Techniques coverage with the Detection coverage dashboard.

  • Quickly identify and fill coverage gaps or filter techniques by adversaries to see the associated coverage.
