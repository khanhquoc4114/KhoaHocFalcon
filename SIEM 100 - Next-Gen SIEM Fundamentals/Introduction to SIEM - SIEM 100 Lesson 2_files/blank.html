Summary

In this module, you learned what SIEM is, why it's essential to modern security operations, and how it supports critical SOC functions like threat detection, investigation, and compliance reporting. You also explored the limitations of traditional SIEMs and why next-generation solutions are reshaping the future of cybersecurity.

  • SIEM stands for Security Information and Event Management—a platform that brings together security data from across your environment to improve visibility, detection, and response.

  • It plays a central role in the SOC, helping analysts stay ahead of threats and make faster, smarter decisions.

  • SIEM supports key use cases like threat detection, compliance reporting, and forensic investigations—all essential to modern security operations.

  • While traditional SIEMs helped shape the field, their limits in scale, speed, and cloud readiness have opened the door for more advanced, Next-Gen SIEM solutions.
