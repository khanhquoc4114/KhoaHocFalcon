Conclusion

This module covered Falcon Onum's architecture, focusing on its efficient edge data processing system. The design emphasizes speed, resource optimization, and real-time threat detection. Here are some key takeaways:

  • •

    Understand the role of distributors and workers. They are essential for efficient edge data processing.

  • •

    Recognize the importance of listeners. They act as entry points for data into the system.

  • •

    Identify the function of pipelines. They transform and route data to its final destination.

  • •

    Acknowledge the significance of the cloud console. It manages clusters and client's data. This is where we can create/modify/delete our pipelines.

Keep these principles in mind to maximize the efficiency and security of Falcon Onum's architecture.
