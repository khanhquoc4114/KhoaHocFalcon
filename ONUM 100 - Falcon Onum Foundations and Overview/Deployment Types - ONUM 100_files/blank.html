Implementation Models

Falcon Onum's implementation model depends primarily on the client's needs and requirements. Whether due to security policies, compliance, or infrastructure constraints within their organization, Falcon Onum can be adapted to on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments. This helps balance costs, enhance control, improve performance, or just address any regulatory restrictions.
