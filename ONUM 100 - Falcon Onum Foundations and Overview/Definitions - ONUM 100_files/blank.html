Conclusion

This module focused on understanding key terminology related to the Falcon Onum platform. Here are some key takeaways:

  • •

    Understand terminology. Familiarity with key terms is crucial for effective platform interaction.

    Recognize the role of labels. Labels help in organizing and filtering data streams efficiently.

    Identify data flow components. Knowing the functions of listeners, distributors, and workers is essential for data processing.
